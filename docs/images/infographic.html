WORLDSTATE & STRESS

18 Capped Variables (key caps)

totalAssigned ≤ 15 • deepWork ≤ 5 • quickWin ≤ 5 • maintenance ≤ 5
deferred ≤ 10 • delegated = 0 • urgentUnassigned ≤ 10
contradictory ≤ 5 • touchedToday ≤ 10 • afterHours ≤ 5
stale ≤ 10 • mysteryMeat ≤ 10 • unclearQuickWins ≤ 5

Stress Score — 8 components, max 100

1. Overload: assigned > 7 → min(20, (n−7) × 4)
2. Deep work: > 1 → (n−1) × 10; == 0 && total > 0 → +5
3. Chaos: bucket × 10 (LOW=0, MED=10, HIGH=20, CRIT=30)
4. Context switch: touched > 5 → min(15, (n−5) × 3)
5. Mystery meat: min(10, n × 2)
6. Unclear scope: min(5, n)
7. Sustained pressure: min(15, consecutiveDays × 5)
8. After-hours: min(10, n × 5)

Stress Levels

≥ 70 CRITICAL ≥ 50 HIGH ≥ 30 MODERATE < 30 LOW
